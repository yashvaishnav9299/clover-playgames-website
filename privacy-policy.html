1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Clover Play Games ("Clover Play Games", "we", "us", or "our"). We build mobile games, digital entertainment experiences, and operate the website https://cloverplaygames.com/ (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you play our games (available on Google Play, Apple App Store, and other platforms) or visit our website, why we collect it, how it is protected, and how you can exercise your privacy and data deletion rights. By installing, accessing, or using our Services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We minimize data collection to what is strictly necessary to deliver, stabilize, and improve our games.

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Support & Contact Inquiries: When you contact us via info@cloverplaygames.com or our Get in Touch page, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message solely to respond to and resolve your inquiry.
  • Voluntary Feedback & Surveys: Any ratings, bug reports, or feedback you voluntarily submit.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically in Our Games

  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Level progression, unlocked achievements, high scores, in-game settings, and session duration.
  • Device & Technical Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version (iOS/Android), language setting, screen resolution, IP address (used for approximate country/region detection and security), crash logs, and performance telemetry.
  • Advertising & App Identifiers: Resettable mobile identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID (AAID) on Android, Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) on iOS (subject to your explicit App Tracking Transparency permission), and app-set installation IDs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process the collected information for the following legitimate business and operational purposes:

  • Operate & Improve Games: Saving your game progress, optimizing frame rates, balancing level difficulty, and fixing software crashes.
  • Player Support: Answering technical questions, restoring lost progress, and resolving billing or gameplay issues.
  • Analytics & Stability: Understanding aggregate, anonymized player engagement patterns to create better game updates.
  • Advertising: Displaying contextual or (where consented) personalized advertisements in free-to-play titles so our games can remain free to download and play.
  • Safety, Security & Anti-Fraud: Detecting cheating, unauthorized modifications, fraudulent transactions, or abuse of our Services.

4. Advertising, Analytics & Third-Party SDKs

Our mobile games may integrate industry-standard third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) for analytics, crash reporting, cloud saves, and advertising. These partners process data under their own privacy policies and applicable data protection agreements:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services: Advertising delivery, Google Play Games sign-in, achievements, and cloud save (Google Privacy Policy ↗).
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crashlytics): Crash reporting and aggregated gameplay telemetry (Firebase Privacy & Security ↗).
  • Unity Technologies (Unity Engine, Unity Analytics & Unity Ads): Game engine runtime, performance diagnostics, and in-game video ads (Unity Player Privacy Policy ↗).
  • Apple Game Center / App Store Services: Leaderboards, achievements, and iOS platform services (Apple Privacy Policy ↗).

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your mobile device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

5. In-App Purchases & Financial Transactions

Some of our games may offer optional virtual items or ad-free upgrades via In-App Purchases (IAP). All financial transactions are processed directly and securely by the platform provider (Google Play Billing or Apple App Store). Clover Play Games never receives, processes, or stores your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing credentials. We only receive an anonymized transaction receipt token to verify and unlock your purchased in-game content.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Protections)

Protecting the privacy of young players is a top priority for Clover Play Games. We comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and equivalent child protection regulations worldwide:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information (such as real names, home addresses, phone numbers, or precise geolocation) from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable European jurisdictions) without verifiable parental consent.
  • Where a game or age-screen identifies a player as a child, third-party ad networks are restricted strictly to non-personalized, contextual, family-safe content.
  • If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe your child has inadvertently provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately at info@cloverplaygames.com. We will promptly verify and permanently delete such information from our records.

7. Data Retention & How to Request Account / Data Deletion

We retain personal or device-associated data only for as long as necessary to provide our Services, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

How to Request Deletion of Your Data (Google Play & App Store Data Safety): You have the right to request the complete deletion of any personal data, support correspondence, or player identifiers associated with you at any time:

  1. Visit our Get in Touch — Privacy & Data Deletion Request page, or send an email directly to info@cloverplaygames.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include the name of the game, your device platform (Android or iOS), and (if applicable) your Player ID or Advertising ID so we can locate any associated records.
  3. We will acknowledge your request promptly and permanently erase your data within 30 days, except where retention is strictly required by law.
  4. To delete local save data stored on your phone or tablet at any time, simply uninstall the game or clear the app's storage in your device settings.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA & Global)

Depending on your region (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, California, India DPDP Act, and Brazil LGPD), you have the right to:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction (Rectification): Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request permanent deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw Consent / Opt-Out: Withdraw consent to personalized advertising at any time via in-game consent dialogs (where applicable) or your device's privacy settings. We do not sell your personal contact information to third parties.

9. Changes to This Policy & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as we launch new games or adapt to new legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
